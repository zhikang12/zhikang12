Working Groups

A framework for focused academic collaboration.

The topic areas below are proposed information-architecture categories. They are not presented as active working groups until their scope, governance, and membership are confirmed.

Working Groups Overview

Standards for future working groups.

Scope and objectives

A concise description of the clinical or methodological problem and intended contribution.

Membership and governance

Verified chairs, members, affiliations, selection process, disclosures, and decision-making arrangements.

Methods and outputs

Planned methods, timelines, protocols, evidence products, publications, and update responsibilities.
