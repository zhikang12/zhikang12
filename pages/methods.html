Methods

Transparent methods for trustworthy clinical pharmacology evidence.

This page defines the intended methodological scope. Detailed protocols and manuals will be added only when reviewed and approved.

Core methods

Methodological areas.

GRADE

Certainty of evidence, recommendation strength, and transparent judgments.

Evidence synthesis

Systematic reviews, meta-analysis, risk-of-bias assessment, and interpretation.

Evidence-to-Decision

Structured consideration of benefits, harms, values, resources, equity, acceptability, and feasibility.

Clinical pharmacology

Methods relevant to dosing, exposure-response relationships, safety, monitoring, and variation in treatment response.

Guideline development

Transparent processes for questions, evidence review, panel judgments, recommendations, and updating.

Reproducibility

Protocol registration, data management, reporting standards, and open-science practices where appropriate.

Protocols forthcomingApproved protocols, templates, and operating procedures have not yet been published.
