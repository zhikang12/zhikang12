Mission

Advance evidence-based clinical pharmacology.

The intended mission is to support rigorous evidence synthesis, transparent methods, education, and trustworthy clinical guidance related to medicines and their use.

This mission statement should be formally reviewed and approved before being treated as an official organizational statement.

Vision

Connect pharmacology evidence with better decisions.

The intended vision is an international academic community in which clinical pharmacology decisions are informed by transparent, current, and appropriately interpreted evidence.

This vision statement remains a working description pending organizational approval.

Core Principles

Standards for future academic work.

  • TransparencyMethods, judgments, authorship, funding, and conflicts of interest should be disclosed when applicable.
  • Methodological rigorEvidence products should follow appropriate, reproducible, and clearly reported methods.
  • Clinical relevanceWork should address meaningful decisions involving medicines, patient outcomes, and health care practice.
  • International collaborationParticipation should support diverse perspectives without implying unconfirmed affiliations or representation.
  • Responsible communicationVerified outputs should be clearly distinguished from proposals, drafts, and placeholder content.
