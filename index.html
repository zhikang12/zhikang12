Scope

A focused evidence platform for clinical pharmacology.

The site is structured around the needs of researchers, clinicians, guideline developers, educators, and collaborators working at the intersection of medicines and evidence-based health care.

  • Clinical pharmacologyMedicines, dosing, monitoring, safety, and variation in treatment response.

Academic design model

Inspired by leading evidence and guideline organizations.

The information architecture draws from public-facing patterns used by groups such as the GRADE Working Group, Cochrane, the International Society for Evidence-Based Health Care, and MAGIC Evidence, while avoiding any implication of formal affiliation.

Reference organization disclaimer References are used for design and information-architecture inspiration only. Confirmed partnerships will be listed on the Partners page when available.
